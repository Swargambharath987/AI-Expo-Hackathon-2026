# Duty Line — Demo Script

3:30 walkthrough for judges.

---

## Landing Page & Profile Setup (0:20)

1. Open the app — Duty Line logo on dark navy background
2. Click **Get Started**
3. Fill in the soldier profile:
   - Name: **Rivera, Maria J.**
   - Rank: **SPC**
   - Grade: **E-4**
   - Unit: **1-503 INF, 82nd ABN**
   - Installation: **Fort Liberty**
   - Supervisor: **SGT Johnson**
4. Click **Continue**

*"This profile auto-fills every form. Set it once, never re-type again."*

---

## Agent 1: TDY Travel (1:00)

Click the **TDY Travel** tab.

**Prompt 1:**
> What's the per diem rate for Columbus, Georgia?

*Instant lookup from cached GSA data.*

**Prompt 2:**
> I need to send SPC Rivera there for 5 days starting July 10, driving POV from Fort Liberty. What's the total cost?

*Cost breakdown — lodging, meals (75% travel day rule), mileage.*

**Prompt 3:**
> Generate the DD 1610 for this trip.

*PDF download appears. Click Download PDF.*

### PDF Walkthrough (0:20)

Open the downloaded DD 1610. Walk through what's filled:
- Traveler name, rank, unit pulled from profile
- Destination, dates, mode of transport from the conversation
- Cost estimate pre-calculated from the tool

*"What normally takes 2-4 hours — looking up rates, doing math, finding the form, filling it out — done in 60 seconds."*

---

## Agent 2: Leave / HR (0:30)

Click the **Leave / HR** tab.

**Prompt 1:**
> I need 10 days ordinary leave starting June 15 to visit family in Texas. Am I eligible?

*Regulation check with AR 600-8-10 citation.*

**Prompt 2:**
> Fill the DA 31.

*PDF download appears. Profile fields already populated.*

*"One message to check eligibility, one message to get the form. 1-2 hours → 30 seconds."*

---

## Agent 3: Regulations (0:20)

Click the **Regulations** tab.

**Prompt 1:**
> Is the government travel card mandatory for TDY?

*One-line answer with JTR citation.*

**Prompt 2:**
> What happens if I use my GTC for personal expenses?

*Follow-up answered with regulation source — the agent understands context.*

*"No more flipping through 1,000 pages. Cited answer in 5 seconds."*

---

## Agent 4: Evaluations (0:20)

Click the **Evaluations** tab.

**Prompt 1:**
> Write NCOER bullets for a squad leader who improved Table VI pass rate from 60% to 95%.

*Bullets that lead with a measurable result. (AR 623-3 isn't in the library yet, so don't claim Duty Line is citing it.)*

*"The hardest part of writing an eval — done in 10 seconds."*

---

## Closing (0:20)

"Every tab you just saw replaces hours of manual work for a different part of a soldier's admin burden. TDY planning, leave requests, regulation lookups, evaluation writing — Duty Line handles all of it with citations and hands you a completed PDF. Across 3 million service members, that's millions of hours returned to mission readiness every year."
